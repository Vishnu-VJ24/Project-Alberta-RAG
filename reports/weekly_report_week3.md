### Research

#### Link to Article

https://www.cn.ca/en/

#### Title of the Article

CN Railway Network

#### Summary of Report

- Reviewed the CN network overview to confirm that CN mainline freight tracks run through Red Deer and the Lacombe County region, providing an existing right-of-way that could support a passenger or tourist rail service to Sylvan Lake.
- Noted that active freight rail infrastructure removes the need for new track construction, which the project partner confirmed is the critical viability factor.
- Identified that CN and CPKC lines together provide coverage across the Central Alberta corridor zone, making this the only zone with existing rail infrastructure.

#### Relation to Project

- Directly supports the Sylvan Lake rail transportation opportunity analysis.
- Confirms the infrastructure precondition for a tourist or commuter shuttle service between Red Deer County and Sylvan Lake.
- Establishes the Central Alberta zone as the priority area for rail-linked transportation improvement recommendations.

#### Motivation for Research

- Needed to verify that active CN rail infrastructure exists along the proposed Sylvan Lake route.
- Needed to understand the network geography before developing the visitor conversion economic model.
- Needed to distinguish between zones with active rail potential and those requiring road-based transportation solutions.

## Tuesday, July 8, 2026

Built the Sylvan Lake transportation opportunity analysis based on the stakeholder framework provided by the project partner. Canadian Pacific Kansas City — Network and Operations

#### Summary of Report

- Confirmed that CPKC lines supplement the CN mainline in this zone, providing additional right-of-way options for a tourist or commuter rail service.
